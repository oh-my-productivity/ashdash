# Code of conduct

Ashdash is a small project made by people who like keyboards and quiet software. We want contributing to it to be a calm,
useful experience for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

## What we expect

* Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
* Keep feedback specific and actionable. Say what you saw, what you expected and, if you can, what would help.
* Respect people's time. Search existing issues first, give a minimal reproduction, and answer questions you were asked.
* Welcome newcomers. A short, friendly explanation is better than a link to a manual.
* Keep private things private: do not post other people's personal information, and report security problems privately
  ([.github/SECURITY.md](.github/SECURITY.md)).

## What we do not accept

* Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments, including about someone's identity.
* Sexualised language or images, and unwelcome attention.
* Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
* Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, and deliberately disruptive behaviour.

## Where it applies

In the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a
problem, use GitHub's "Report content" feature on the comment, or contact a maintainer through the email address on their
GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence. This document is short on purpose; maintainers use judgement and will try
to be fair and to explain their decisions.
